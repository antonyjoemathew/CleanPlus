About CleanPlus Laundry

A brand-new laundry and dry-cleaning store in Kakkanad, built around one simple promise: fresh clothes, every time, with none of the usual hassle.

Fresh ideas, spotless results

CleanPlus Laundry opened its doors near CSI Church, Chittethukara with a simple belief: laundry should be the easiest errand of your week. That's why everything starts with a WhatsApp message. No apps to download, no accounts to create, no queues.

Behind the counter, we take the craft seriously. Clothes are sorted by fabric and colour, treated for stains individually, cleaned with gentle skin-safe products and finished with proper pressing. From everyday office formals to wedding silks, sneakers to bike helmets, every item gets the care it was made for.
